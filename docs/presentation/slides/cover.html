Efficient proof of training steps

Pacif.ai

• Topic: how an independent auditor can confirm that an AI model was trained exactly as claimed, on the agreed data, step by step. • Why it matters: a trainer could secretly slip harmful data (a "backdoor") into training. Today, checking for that is expensive or only statistical. • The promise of this talk: a check that covers every step and the whole model, gives a mathematical guarantee, and costs the auditor far less than re-training.
